    DAILY ACTIVITY
    PRODUCTIVITY TREND
    What is this? A day-by-day "score" of your activity, whether or not you used the focus timer. Score = (tasks completed, weighted by priority A→E, ×10) + (minutes of timer-tracked focus, if any). So a heavy task day still shows up even with 0 timer minutes.
    Peak (▲) — a day that scored higher than the days beside it. Your "sweet spot" — worth noticing what you did differently that day.
    Low (▼) — a day that scored lower than the days beside it. Not automatically bad — could be a planned rest day.
    Slope — the line between two days. Green = increasing, red = decreasing, gray = flat (steady/no change).
    Inflection (◆) — a point where the trend's curvature flips, e.g. from "slowing down" to "speeding up." A hint that momentum is changing direction, often before a peak or low actually shows up.
    Tap any point on the graph for a plain-language breakdown of that day and the interval around it.
